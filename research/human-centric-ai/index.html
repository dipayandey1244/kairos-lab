Human-Centric AI

Exploring systems whose usefulness depends on human goals, interpretation and meaningful interaction.

01

Design around people

Human-centric AI treats the human context as part of the technical problem: what people are trying to accomplish, how they interpret system outputs, where uncertainty matters and how interaction changes outcomes.

human goal → system behavior → human interpretation → outcome
02

Research questions

Interpretability

How can users understand what a model knows and does not know?

Interaction

How does system behavior change when humans are in the loop?

Usefulness

How should technical performance connect to real user outcomes?
