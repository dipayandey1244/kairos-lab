RESEARCH THEME · 03

Vision-Language-Action

Connecting visual perception, language instructions and action in embodied systems.

01

One loop

An embodied agent must connect what it sees, what it is asked to do, what it believes about the environment and what action it should take. VLA research studies the representations and learning objectives that make this loop coherent.

observation + instruction → representation → action
