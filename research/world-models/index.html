World Models

Learning structured representations that support prediction, simulation and planning.

01

Why world models?

A world model aims to represent enough of an environment's structure to predict how observations may evolve under actions. The useful abstraction is not merely a compressed image; it is a representation that can support reasoning about possible futures.
