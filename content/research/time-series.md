# Time Series & Dynamical Systems

## The Manifold Hypothesis in Complex Systems

High-dimensional physical systems—ranging from power grids and neural networks to turbulent fluid flows—often appear intractable when analyzed degree by degree. However, phase-space trajectories in dissipative systems contract rapidly onto low-dimensional invariant manifolds.

> "Microscopic high dimensionality gives rise to macroscopic low-dimensional simplicity."

---

## 3 Core Paradigm Systems

1. **Kuramoto Oscillators**: 200 phase variables collapsing into 5 synchronized phase clusters.
2. **Stuart–Landau Systems**: 200 complex nonlinear oscillators organizing into 7 limit-cycle clusters on the complex plane.
3. **Kuramoto–Sivashinsky PDE**: 200 spatial points evolving along a 5–10 dimensional chaotic attractor.
