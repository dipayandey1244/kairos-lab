# Mathematical Foundations of Prediction

## Predictability Horizons & Lyapunov Exponents

Nonlinear dynamical systems exhibit sensitive dependence on initial conditions quantified by the maximal Lyapunov exponent $\lambda_{\max}$. The characteristic predictability time scale (Lyapunov time) is given by:

$$\tau_{\text{pred}} \sim \frac{1}{\lambda_{\max}} \ln \left( \frac{\epsilon_{\text{tol}}}{\epsilon_0} \right)$$

By training foundation transformers on discrete representations of continuous attractors, we leverage topological shadow dynamics to forecast beyond classical linear error growth limits.
