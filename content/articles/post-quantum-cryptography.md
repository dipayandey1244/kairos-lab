# Post-Quantum Cryptography in Distributed Ledger Systems

## Introduction

Shor's algorithm poses an existential threat to classical public-key cryptography, including RSA and Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECDSA). As fault-tolerant quantum computers approach practical thresholds, distributed networks must transition to lattice-based post-quantum standards.

> "Quantum resistance cannot be patched overnight. Transitioning core financial ledgers to lattice-based signatures requires multi-year cryptographic migration phases."

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Signature Size Overhead**: Dilithium and Falcon signatures require up to 8x larger storage footprint than ECDSA.
- **Lattice Hardness Assumptions**: Learning With Errors (LWE) problems remain resilient against known quantum algorithms.
- **Migration Timelines**: Institutional financial networks must adopt PQC algorithms by 2028 to prevent store-now-decrypt-later attacks.

---

## Conclusion

Preparing global financial networks for the post-quantum era requires proactive architectural migration. Integrating PQC primitives today ensures long-term cryptographic integrity.
