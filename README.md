# KAIROS RESEARCH LAB

A premium editorial research site for long-form explanations, mathematical notes and experiments around intelligent systems.

## Research themes

- **Time Series Foundation Models** — temporal modeling, learned representations, transformers, foundation models and controlled dynamical systems.
- **World Models** — structured representations for prediction, simulation and planning.
- **Vision-Language-Action** — connecting perception, language and action for embodied systems.
- **Embodied AI** — intelligence grounded in interaction, control and environments.
- **Human-Centric AI** — systems designed around human goals and meaningful use.

## Time Series chapter

The Time Series Foundation Models page includes a step-by-step path from statistical foundations through transformers and foundation models, then connects those ideas to the WAM oscillator environments supplied for the research work.

## Design language

Warm paper background, deep navy typography, restrained crimson accents, Cormorant Garamond display type and Inter UI type. The generated hero visual is stored under `assets/hero.jpg`.


## AI Engineering Academy

The Academy is a staged, research-backed learning workspace for LLM systems, PyTorch, agents, orchestration, evaluation engineering and reproducible AI research.

- **Open the Academy:** [AI Engineering Academy](./ai-engineering/)
- **Implementation roadmap:** [AI Engineering Academy plan](./docs/AI_ENGINEERING_ACADEMY_PLAN.md)

The first milestone delivers Module 0: four lessons covering AI engineering roles, the AI product lifecycle, model/application/agent/orchestrator boundaries, and a practical diagnostic architecture lab. Lesson progress and reading marks are stored locally in the browser. Later modules are explicitly labeled as planned until their full lessons are implemented.
