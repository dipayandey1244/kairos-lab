# KAIROS RESEARCH LAB

A premium editorial research site for long-form explanations, mathematical notes and experiments around intelligent systems.

## Research themes

- **Time Series Foundation Models** — temporal modeling, learned representations, transformers, foundation models and controlled dynamical systems.
- **World Models** — structured representations for prediction, simulation and planning.
- **Vision-Language-Action** — connecting perception, language and action for embodied systems.
- **Embodied AI** — intelligence grounded in interaction, control and environments.
- **Human-Centric AI** — systems designed around human goals and meaningful use.

## Time Series chapter

The Time Series Foundation Models page includes a step-by-step path from statistical foundations through transformers and foundation models, then connects those ideas to the WAM oscillator environments supplied for the research work.
