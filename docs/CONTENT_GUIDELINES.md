# Content Guidelines - KAIROS Platform

## 1. Writing Style

Articles should maintain an authoritative yet accessible editorial tone:
- **Clear**: Prefer direct sentences and active verbs.
- **Evidence-Based**: Back analytical claims with empirical findings or references.
- **Concise**: Avoid fluff, excessive jargon, and repetitive intro paragraphs.

---

## 2. Paragraph & Heading Rules

- **Paragraph Length**: Keep paragraphs between 3–5 sentences for optimal digital reading readability.
- **Heading Hierarchy**: Always use `## H2` for primary article sections and `### H3` for subsections. Never skip heading levels.

---

## 3. Formatting Standards

- **Key Takeaways**: Include a bulleted key takeaways block after the introduction or before the conclusion.
- **Pull Quotes**: Use blockquotes `> Quote text` for pivotal thesis statements.
- **Citations**: Number references sequentially in brackets (e.g., `[1]`) and list full citations in the References section.
